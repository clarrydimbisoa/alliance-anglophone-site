About Alliance Anglophone

A learning initiative connecting practical English, employability, professional confidence, and access to opportunity.

Who we are

Alliance Anglophone is being developed in Toamasina, Madagascar, as an online-first English and employability initiative. Our programmes are designed to connect language learning with communication, workplace readiness, and real professional goals.

We are building the organisation progressively. We publish only roles that are genuinely assigned and only team profiles whose information has been checked and whose owner has agreed to publication.

How we work

Practical learning

English is taught for real communication, study, work, and professional development.

Respect and inclusion

Learners, volunteers, partners, and team members are treated with dignity.

Transparency

Prices, programme status, responsibilities, and claims are communicated accurately.

Responsible visibility

A person’s profile, image, voice, or story is published only with informed permission.

Our team

The public team list begins with the founding coordinator. Additional members and volunteers will be added after their role, profile information, and publication preferences have been confirmed.

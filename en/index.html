Questions before you begin

What are the new tuition fees?

Individual General English is 90,000 Ar per month. Technical and/or Business English is 125,000 Ar per month.

How does the trial week work?

It is free: WhatsApp lessons and exercises from 7 to 11 September, followed by a group video session on Saturday, 12 September at 10:00.

What is the deadline?

Registration and trial-participation requests must be sent by Thursday, 3 September 2026.

What does General English include?

Two 90-minute live lessons each week, a weekly English Speaking Club, and 60 to 90 minutes of guided independent practice.

When do I pay?

There is no payment for the trial. For enrollment, wait for written confirmation of the program, amount, and terms.

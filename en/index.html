Questions before you begin

What are the tuition fees?

Individual General English is 90,000 Ar per month. Technical and/or Business English is 125,000 Ar per month.

Can I request registration at any time?

Yes. Requests are open year-round, subject to availability. The starting date and timetable are confirmed in writing.

What does General English include?

Two 90-minute live lessons each week, a weekly English Speaking Club, and 60 to 90 minutes of guided independent practice.

How do I choose the right program?

Review the programs and prices, then include your level, goal, and availability in the registration request.

When do I pay?

Wait for written confirmation of the program, group, amount, and terms before making any payment.

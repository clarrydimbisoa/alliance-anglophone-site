Before you begin

When does the next cohort begin?

A new online cohort begins on the first Monday of every month. Your place and timetable are confirmed in writing.

How do I choose the right program?

Start with the pathway that matches your goal. If you are unsure, describe your need in the request and we will help orient you.

When should I pay?

Only after receiving written confirmation of the program, group, amount, and applicable terms.

Do you use the CEFR?

Yes. General English uses CEFR-informed progression with observable communicative goals. Alliance Anglophone does not award an official CEFR qualification.

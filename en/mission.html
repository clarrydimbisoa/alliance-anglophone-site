Our Mission

Alliance Anglophone is built to turn English skills into opportunity—through structured learning, professional pathways, and measurable progress.

Vision

To build a strong community of English-capable learners and professionals in Madagascar, ready to access national and international opportunities.

Mission

Provide professional English training that is accessible, career-oriented, and aligned with international communication standards—so learners can study, work, and grow with confidence.

Values

  • Excellence: serious learning, clear standards, real progress.
  • Practical outcomes: English that serves careers and real life.
  • Respect: supportive learning, no shame, no pressure.
  • Consistency: discipline, structure, and long-term growth.
  • Opportunity: skills that open doors.

Our Promise

We do not sell “English lessons.” We deliver structured programs with measurable outcomes: speaking confidence, vocabulary growth, pronunciation improvement, and career readiness.

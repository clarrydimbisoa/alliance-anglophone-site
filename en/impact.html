Impact framework: from English to opportunity

Alliance Anglophone publishes its measurement framework first. Numerical results will appear only after they have been collected and verified.

Current status: measurement framework defined; data collection and validation are being prepared.

Our impact logic

Practical English → professional readiness → access to opportunity → career growth.

Indicators we will track

Participation

Enrollment, attendance, and program completion.

Progress

Pre/post assessment, speaking, comprehension, and communicative confidence.

Employability

Completed CVs, mock interviews, and professional skills practised.

Opportunity access

Verified access to employment, internships, training, or professional opportunities.

Retention

Follow-up over time when learners consent to being contacted.

Satisfaction

Learner and partner feedback, with sample size stated.

Evidence rules

No invented numbers

When data are not yet available, we say so clearly.

Quantitative + qualitative

Indicators can be complemented by learner stories published with consent and verifiable evidence.

Context stated

Each published result will identify the period, program, and number of people concerned.

Useful measurement for partners

An organization can define expected outcomes before training and receive a progress summary appropriate to the program.

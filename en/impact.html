Impact Through English and Employability

Alliance Anglophone measures impact by connecting English learning to confidence, job readiness, income opportunities, and sustainable workforce integration.

Our impact logic

English is not the final destination. For Alliance Anglophone, English is a practical tool that can support employability, income generation, professional confidence, and long-term career development.

Our model is designed to help learners move from language ability to real-world outcomes.

The impact chain

Our work follows a simple but powerful transformation pathway.

1

English Skills

Practical speaking, listening, reading, writing, vocabulary, and professional communication.

2

Job Readiness

CV preparation, interview practice, workplace behavior, soft skills, and confidence.

3

Opportunity Access

Better readiness for employment, training, internships, service jobs, or remote work.

4

Career Growth

Improved confidence, income opportunities, retention, and long-term professional mobility.

What we aim to measure

Impact should be tracked, not only claimed. Alliance Anglophone is preparing a simple monitoring system.

01

Participation

Number of learners enrolled and number of learners completing programs.

02

Learning progress

Improvement in practical English level, confidence, and ability to communicate.

03

Employability

CVs created, interviews practiced, applications submitted, and job-readiness skills developed.

04

Opportunity access

Access to employment, further training, internships, career pathways, or professional opportunities.

05

Retention

Follow-up on learners who access employment or training and their ability to remain engaged over time.

06

Satisfaction

Participant satisfaction, confidence improvement, and feedback from learners or partners.

Social impact focus

The social value of Alliance Anglophone is strongest where English training becomes a bridge to better opportunity.

Who we aim to support

  • Young people seeking employability skills
  • Job seekers needing English for better opportunities
  • Workers who need professional communication
  • Learners in transition who need confidence and structure
  • Organizations seeking workforce development

What we aim to improve

  • Practical English communication
  • Job-readiness and professional confidence
  • Access to employment or further training
  • Income opportunities through better communication
  • Long-term learning habits and career mobility

Partnership value

Our impact model is useful for companies, NGOs, institutions, and projects that need measurable workforce development.

For companies

Better-prepared candidates and employees with stronger communication, confidence, and workplace readiness.

For NGOs and projects

Training that can support employability, youth development, reintegration, and professional communication.

For institutions

A practical model linking training, outcomes, reporting, and long-term learning infrastructure.

Monitoring and reporting

Alliance Anglophone is preparing a simple monitoring and evaluation approach to track learning and employability outcomes.

Tools we plan to use

  • Learner registration records
  • Pre/post English level checks
  • Completion tracking
  • Follow-up surveys
  • Employer or partner feedback when available

Reports we can prepare

  • Training completion summaries
  • Progress reports
  • Participant feedback summaries
  • Employment or training access follow-up
  • Partner-oriented impact notes

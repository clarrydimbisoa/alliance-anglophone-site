Become a Volunteer

Help transform English skills into career opportunities. Join Alliance Anglophone and contribute to education, employability, and community development in Madagascar.

Why volunteer with us?

Alliance Anglophone is building a learning platform that connects English education with professional growth. Volunteers play a key role in supporting learners, creating resources, mentoring students, promoting opportunities, and expanding access to quality English education.

English Mentor

Support learners through conversation practice, speaking activities, and confidence building.

Trainer & Facilitator

Share your expertise in English, communication, leadership, employability, or career development.

Content Creator

Help create lessons, exercises, videos, articles, and learning resources.

Community Ambassador

Promote Alliance Anglophone programs, events, and opportunities in your network.

Career Coach

Assist learners with CV writing, interview preparation, and workplace readiness.

Partner Volunteer

Represent organizations, institutions, or companies interested in collaboration.

Expected impact

  • Improve access to practical English education.
  • Increase employability and career readiness.
  • Support students, job seekers, and professionals.
  • Create opportunities for international collaboration.
  • Strengthen community development through education.

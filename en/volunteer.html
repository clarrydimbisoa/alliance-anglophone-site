Become a Volunteer

Help transform English skills into career opportunities. Join Alliance Anglophone and contribute to education, employability, and community development in Madagascar.

Why volunteer with Alliance Anglophone?

Alliance Anglophone is building a learning platform that connects English education with professional growth. Volunteers can support learners, create resources, mentor students, promote opportunities, and help expand access to practical English education.

Whether you are a teacher, professional, student, communicator, mentor, or partner, your contribution can help learners become more confident and better prepared for real opportunities.

Volunteer roles

You can support the mission according to your skills, availability, and experience.

English Mentor

Support learners through conversation practice, speaking activities, pronunciation, and confidence building.

Trainer & Facilitator

Share your expertise in English, communication, leadership, employability, or career development.

Content Creator

Help create lessons, exercises, videos, worksheets, social media posts, and learning resources.

Community Ambassador

Promote Alliance Anglophone programs, events, and opportunities in your network and community.

Career Coach

Assist learners with CV writing, interview preparation, workplace communication, and professional confidence.

Partner Volunteer

Represent organizations, institutions, or companies interested in collaboration, training, or impact support.

Expected impact

  • Improve access to practical English education.
  • Increase employability and career readiness.
  • Support students, job seekers, workers, and professionals.
  • Create opportunities for mentoring and international collaboration.
  • Strengthen community development through education and communication.

Who can volunteer?

You do not need to be perfect. You need to be serious, respectful, reliable, and willing to contribute to a meaningful learning mission.

English speakers

Native or non-native English speakers who can help learners practice and improve.

Teachers and trainers

Educators who can contribute methods, materials, lessons, or coaching.

Professionals

People with experience in business, tourism, NGOs, customer service, logistics, maritime, or career development.

Students

Motivated students who want to support learning activities, communication, and community outreach.

Organizations

Groups, companies, churches, associations, NGOs, or institutions that want to collaborate.

Digital contributors

Volunteers who can help with content, design, video, social media, online learning, or administration.

Whether you can contribute one hour per month or several hours per week, your support can make a meaningful difference.
